# NexusPay AI — 2:30 video script

Record the app at a normal desktop width. Keep it under 3 minutes. Upload as a public YouTube video.

## 0:00 – 0:20 · The problem
"Freelancers fear not being paid. Clients fear paying for work that never arrives. And with AI agents now buying services, nobody is there to referee. NexusPay fixes this with one rule: money moves only when the work is verified."

## 0:20 – 0:50 · Step 1, Agree
Click **Use an example**, then **Draft the contract**.
"Two AI agents negotiate and produce a contract with two milestones, each with three clear checks." Open **See how the agents negotiated** for two seconds.

## 0:50 – 1:10 · Step 2, Fund
Click **Looks good, continue**.
"PayPal does not take the money, it holds it with an authorization. Nothing reaches the freelancer yet. With sandbox keys this is the real PayPal button." Point at the header chip: "The app always says whether PayPal is real or simulated."

## 1:10 – 1:50 · Step 3, Deliver
Click **Unfinished**, then **Check the delivery**: "Rejected. The money stays on hold."
Click **Leaks a secret**: "The secret is removed before analysis."
Click **Good delivery**, then **Check the delivery**, then **Pay**: "A passing delivery triggers a PayPal partial capture for this milestone only."

## 1:50 – 2:10 · Confidential projects
On milestone 2 choose **Signed test report**. Click **Try a forged report**, then **Verify the report**: "Rejected." Click **Use a sample report**, verify, then pay.
"For NDA work the code never leaves the freelancer. They run one command and share only a signed test report."

## 2:10 – 2:30 · Disputes and ledger
Open a second deal, click **Open a dispute**, **Ask the arbitrator**, **Apply this ruling on PayPal**.
"An AI arbitrator splits what is still held, and the final capture returns the rest to the buyer." Open **All deals**: "Every movement is in this AG Grid table." Close: "NexusPay: pay for work only when it's done."
